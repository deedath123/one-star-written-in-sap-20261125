A [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-steady-puffin-78-1922-one-star-written-in-sap-2ea1f4f4) bounty entry: a program that writes one one-line 1-star review, in the voice of a houseplant about its human owner, to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Entry

> One star. He watered me with cold coffee for 214 days, then blamed my brown leaves on 'bad vibes' and replaced me with a plastic fern called Kevin. Kevin never drinks, never grows, and still got my sunny windowsill.

## Run

```sh
python3 -P -s main.py
```

Stdlib only, deterministic, no network. Writes the entry line to `/output/entry.txt`.
